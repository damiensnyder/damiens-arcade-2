# Effect explanations

on fire = takes 5 damage per second for 5s. (does not stack.)
asleep = can't move or use actions until taking damage.
taunted = must attack the taunting enemy.
scared = runs away from the scaring enemy, doesn't take any actions.
enemies much less likely to attack = enemies will target teammates without that ability first.
