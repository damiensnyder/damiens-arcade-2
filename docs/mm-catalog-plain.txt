# Effect explanations

on fire = takes 5 damage per second for 5s. (does not stack.)
stunned = can't move or use actions.
taunted = must attack the taunting enemy.
scared = runs away from the scaring enemy, doesn't take any actions.
enemies much less likely to attack = enemies will target teammates without that ability first.
decoy = copy of the fighter but it has 25 HP, 0 to all stats, no equipment, and doesn't move or attack.

fighter ability names are only for reference, not displayed in game.
all actions without a cooldown have an implicit 1s cooldown.
